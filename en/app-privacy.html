App privacy policy

Effective date: November 27, 2026
Operator: Hinazou Co Ltd
Address: 〒530-0047 大阪府大阪市北区西天満四丁目8番28号
Contact: info@hinazou.jp

Hinazou Co Ltd ("we") takes your personal information seriously. This policy explains what information the translation app "Zou Translate" (the "App") handles and how.

1. Information we handle

(1) Content you provide while using the App

  • What you say (voice) and what you type
  • Sound recorded in Listen-in mode
  • Text recognised from the screen during screen translation
  • The sound in a video you choose to translate

(2) Information needed to provide the service

  • Invitation code
  • Phone ID: a string of random digits the App makes up the first time it is opened, used to confirm that one invitation code is used on only one phone. It is not a telephone number, and it does not contain the phone's serial number, any Apple identifier, your name, or similar information.
  • Usage: how much of the allowance each invitation code has used, and on which day

(3) What we do not collect

  • Name, home address, telephone number, email address (except when you email us yourself)
  • Location, contacts
  • The App shows no ads and uses no analytics or tracking tools.

2. What we use it for

  • To translate what you say, what you type, the text on your screen, and what is said in your video
  • To check that an invitation code is valid, and to calculate and limit usage for each code
  • To prevent misuse (for example, entering wrong invitation codes again and again)
  • To reply to your enquiries

We do not use it for any other purpose.

3. Where the information goes

(1) When you are online

What you say, what you type, the sound in Listen-in mode, the text recognised on your screen, and the sound of a video are sent first to our server (running in Fly.io's Tokyo data centre), and then passed to OpenAI, which turns speech into text and translates it. The result is sent back to your phone.

(2) Screen translation

When you stop screen translation, none of the screen data is kept anywhere other than your phone.

(3) Video translation

The sound of the video is sent for translation; the new dubbed voice is made on your phone. The finished video is kept inside the App, and is saved to your photo library only if you choose "Save to Photos".

4. Who we entrust it to (third-party processors)

To provide translation, we entrust the content in 1 (1) to the following providers:

  • OpenAI (United States): speech-to-text and translation. OpenAI processes it under its own terms.
  • Fly.io (a US company; the server is located in Tokyo, Japan): runs our server.

Except for the above, and where required by law, we do not give your information to anyone else, and we do not sell it.

5. Transfer abroad

OpenAI, mentioned above, is a US company, so your content is sent to the United States for processing. The United States has no personal-information protection system equivalent to Japan's, but OpenAI publishes its own data-protection measures. By using the App's online functions, you agree to the transfer abroad described above.

6. How long we keep it

  • What you say and the sentences translated: our server does not keep them; they are discarded once processed. The server log records only which function was used, whether it succeeded, and how long it took, never the content.
  • Invitation codes and Phone IDs: kept only in an encrypted form from which the original cannot be read (a hash value), until that invitation code is deactivated.
  • Usage records: kept for 62 days.
  • How long OpenAI keeps data follows OpenAI's terms.
  • Content on your phone (conversation history, favourites, Listen-in recordings, the screen-translation cache, finished videos) is kept on your phone and deleted by you. The screen-translation cache is deleted automatically after 30 days. When you delete the App, these are deleted with it (the Phone ID stays in the phone's keychain).

7. Security

Communication between the phone and the server is encrypted. The server does not keep what you say; invitation codes and Phone IDs are kept only in encrypted form.

8. Children

The App is not designed for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Your rights

You may ask us to disclose, correct, stop using, or delete information about you. The only things on the server that can be matched to you are your invitation code, Phone ID and usage. Please send your invitation code to info@hinazou.jp, and we will act after confirming it. Content on your phone you can delete yourself in the App's Settings.

10. Changes to this policy

This policy may be changed. After a change we will publish it on this page, and we will tell you inside the App about important changes.

11. Contact us

Hinazou Co Ltd　info@hinazou.jp

This policy is published in Japanese, Chinese and English. The Japanese version prevails.

Privacy policy

Hinazou Co Ltd (“we”) recognises the importance of the personal information our customers entrust to us. We comply with Japan's Act on the Protection of Personal Information and other related laws, and handle personal information properly under the following policy.

1. Information we collect

When you contact us by e-mail or phone, we may obtain the following information.

  • Your name
  • Company or shop name
  • E-mail address
  • Telephone number
  • What you ask us, and any other information you give us

2. How we use it

We use the personal information we obtain only for the following purposes.

  • To answer your enquiries and to contact you
  • To tell you about our services and make proposals
  • To provide the services you have contracted, and to contact you in connection with them

3. Providing information to third parties

We do not provide personal information to third parties without the consent of the person concerned, except in the following cases.

  • Where required by law
  • Where it is necessary to protect a person's life, body or property and it is difficult to obtain the person's consent
  • Where we need to cooperate with a national or local government body carrying out duties set by law

4. Security

We take the security measures that are necessary and appropriate to prevent personal information from being leaked, lost or damaged. We endeavour to erase without delay personal information we no longer need to use.

5. Requests for disclosure and the like

When a person asks us to disclose, correct, add to, delete or stop using their own personal information, we confirm that the request comes from that person and respond promptly as the law requires. Please send such requests to the contact below.

6. Changes to this policy

We may change this policy when laws are revised or when we review how we handle information. The changed policy applies from the time it is published on this page.

Translation app Zou Translate Beta

Can't follow, can't read, can't reply? One phone handles it all.

A translation app for people living abroad (in beta)

Join the beta

Three features

  • ThemYou
    01

    Face-to-face translation

    It translates and reads aloud as soon as you finish speaking. Put the phone on the table: the screen splits in two, and the other person's half faces them.

  • Original appTranslation
    02

    Screen translation

    On a foldable iPhone, the original app sits on one side and the translation on the other. Turn the page and the translation follows. No screenshots.

  • 03

    Live captions

    Briefings, meetings and long explanations at a counter become captions on the spot. Get a summary of the last few minutes, or just ask, “When is the deadline?”

* The drawings above only show the idea. Pictures of the real screens will be added when they are ready.

Where it helps

  • City hall, hospital and bank counters
  • Apartment hunting
  • School briefings
  • Work meetings
  • Local apps and notices

Also

  • Fix a misheard word

    Say “not A, B” and only that word changes.

  • Favorites

    Save important sentences and come back to them.
